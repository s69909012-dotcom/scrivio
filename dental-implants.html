Understanding Dental Implants

Learn the basics before exploring treatment options.

✦

What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are placed in the jawbone to provide support for a replacement tooth. A dental professional can explain whether this treatment is suitable for your situation.

○

Who May Consider Dental Implants?

Dental implants may be considered by people with one or more missing teeth. Suitability depends on individual oral health, dental needs and professional assessment.

What Does the Treatment Process Involve?

The exact treatment plan can vary from person to person.

1

Consultation

Discuss your dental needs and treatment goals with a dental professional.

2

Treatment Plan

A treatment approach is planned based on your individual situation.

3

Implant Placement

If appropriate, the implant is placed as part of the planned treatment.

4

Restoration

After the required healing period, a replacement tooth may be fitted.
