Dental Implants for Missing Teeth

Dental implants are a fixed tooth-replacement option designed to replace missing teeth and restore everyday function and appearance. Learn about the treatment, available options and what to consider before choosing an implant treatment option.

UNDERSTANDING THE TREATMENT

What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is an artificial tooth root that is placed in the jawbone to support a replacement tooth or dental restoration. The treatment can be considered for people with one or more missing teeth, depending on their individual dental condition.

01

Implant Fixture

The implant fixture acts as the artificial tooth root and is placed within the jawbone.

02

Abutment

The abutment connects the implant fixture with the replacement tooth or restoration.

03

Crown or Restoration

The visible replacement tooth is designed to restore appearance and everyday function.

SUITABILITY

Who May Consider Dental Implants?

Dental implants may be considered by people who have missing teeth and want to explore a fixed tooth-replacement option.

  • People with one or more missing teeth
  • People looking for a fixed tooth-replacement option
  • People exploring alternatives to removable dentures
  • People who want to understand whether dental implants may be suitable for their situation
Important: Suitability depends on factors such as oral health, gum condition, jawbone condition and individual dental circumstances. A dental professional should assess your situation before treatment.

TREATMENT PROCESS

How Does Dental Implant Treatment Work?

Dental implant treatment can involve several stages. The exact approach depends on the patient's individual dental condition and treatment plan.

01

Assessment

The dental condition and suitability for implant treatment are evaluated.

02

Treatment Planning

A suitable treatment approach is planned based on the individual situation.

03

Implant Placement

The implant fixture is placed in the jawbone by a qualified dental professional.

04

Restoration

After the appropriate treatment stages, the replacement tooth or restoration is fitted.

TREATMENT OPTIONS

Dental Implant Options

Dental implant treatment can be planned differently depending on the number of missing teeth and the patient's individual requirements.

Single Tooth Implant

An option that may be considered when replacing a single missing tooth.

Multiple Tooth Replacement

Implant-supported solutions may be considered when several teeth are missing.

Full-Mouth or Full-Arch Options

People with extensive tooth loss may explore implant-supported full-mouth or full-arch replacement options.

POTENTIAL BENEFITS

Why Consider Dental Implants?

Fixed Replacement

Dental implants are designed to support a fixed tooth-replacement restoration.

Natural-Looking Appearance

The visible restoration can be designed to resemble natural teeth.

Everyday Function

Implant-supported teeth can help restore everyday chewing function.

Long-Term Option

With appropriate care and professional follow-up, dental implants can be a long-term tooth-replacement option.

BEFORE CHOOSING TREATMENT

What to Keep in Mind Before Choosing Dental Implants

  • Dental implants are not suitable for everyone.
  • Treatment planning depends on individual dental conditions.
  • Gum and jawbone health can be important considerations.
  • Treatment may involve multiple stages.
  • A dental professional should assess your individual situation before treatment.

COST INFORMATION

Dental Implant Cost

Dental implant treatment costs can vary depending on the number of teeth being replaced, treatment complexity, restoration type and other individual clinical factors.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Dental Implant FAQs

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots placed in the jawbone to support a replacement tooth or dental restoration.

Who can get dental implants?

Dental implants may be considered by people with missing teeth, but suitability depends on individual dental and oral health conditions. A dental professional should assess each case.

Are dental implants suitable for everyone?

No. Suitability can depend on factors including gum health, jawbone condition, existing dental conditions and the individual's overall treatment requirements.

How long does dental implant treatment take?

Treatment time varies between patients and depends on the treatment plan, healing requirements and individual dental circumstances.

How much do dental implants cost?

Dental implant costs vary depending on the number of teeth being replaced, treatment complexity, restoration type and other individual factors.

Are dental implants better than dentures?

Dental implants and dentures are different tooth replacement options. The appropriate option depends on individual dental needs, oral health, treatment suitability and professional assessment.
