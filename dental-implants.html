What is a dental implant?

A dental implant is an artificial tooth-root system placed in the jawbone to support a replacement tooth. The implant fixture, abutment and crown work together as a replacement for a missing tooth.

Understanding the Basics

What Is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant is a tooth-replacement system designed to replace a missing tooth. It typically consists of an implant fixture, an abutment and a replacement crown.

The implant fixture is positioned in the bone, while the abutment connects the fixture with the replacement crown.

Why Replacement May Matter

Missing Tooth Ko Replace Kyun Karein?

A missing tooth can affect more than appearance. Depending on the individual situation, it may influence chewing, bite and the surrounding teeth.

Whether replacement is appropriate depends on your oral condition, the location of the missing tooth and your overall treatment needs.

Replacement Choices

Dental Treatment Options

Dental implants are one of several approaches used to replace missing teeth. The suitable option depends on individual evaluation.

Dental Implant

Implant-supported tooth replacement using an artificial root and replacement crown.

Dental Bridge

A replacement tooth supported by the surrounding teeth.

Removable Denture

A removable replacement option for one or more missing teeth.

Dental Implant Benefits

Why Consider Dental Implants?

Dental implants can offer several characteristics that make them an option for tooth replacement in suitable cases.

Artificial Root Support

Provides support through an implant fixture placed in bone.

Fixed Replacement

A replacement crown can be attached to the implant system.

Helps Restore Chewing

Tooth replacement may help restore chewing function.

Natural-Looking Crown

A replacement crown can be designed to resemble a natural tooth.

Complete Implant Structure

A complete dental implant restoration generally involves three main components: the implant fixture, the abutment and the crown.

  • Crown: the visible replacement tooth.
  • Abutment: connects the crown to the fixture.
  • Implant Fixture: the artificial root component.

What Is Osseointegration?

Osseointegration refers to the process in which bone develops a close relationship with the implant surface during healing.

Things to Consider

Dental Implant Risks & Downsides

! Things to Consider
  • Implant treatment involves a surgical procedure.
  • Healing time can vary between individuals.
  • Bone and gum condition can influence treatment planning.
  • Good oral hygiene and ongoing maintenance are important.
  • Not every missing tooth situation is suitable for an implant.
