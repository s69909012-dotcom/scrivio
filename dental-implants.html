What are dental implants?

Dental implants are a tooth-replacement option for people with one or more missing teeth. A typical implant-supported restoration includes an implant fixture, an abutment and a dental crown or another suitable restoration. Whether implants are appropriate depends on professional assessment.

UNDERSTANDING DENTAL IMPLANTS

How a Dental Implant Is Structured

A dental implant restoration is made up of components that work together to replace the visible tooth and provide support below the gumline.

01

Implant Fixture

The implant fixture is placed into the jawbone and can provide the foundation for an implant-supported restoration.

02

Abutment

The abutment connects the implant fixture with the restoration positioned above the gumline.

03

Dental Crown

A dental crown or another suitable restoration can be attached to complete the tooth-replacement structure.

MISSING TOOTH REPLACEMENT

Dental Implants for Missing Teeth

Missing teeth can make everyday activities such as eating and speaking less comfortable. Dental implants are one option that may be considered when planning tooth replacement.

The appropriate approach depends on factors such as the number and location of missing teeth, gum condition, jawbone condition and the individual's overall oral health.

✓

One missing tooth: A single implant-supported restoration may be considered.

✓

Several missing teeth: Multiple implants or another suitable replacement approach may be considered.

✓

Extensive tooth loss: Implant-supported full-arch solutions may be considered in selected situations.

i

Important to Note

Dental implant treatment is not the same for everyone. A qualified dental professional needs to assess the mouth, gums and jawbone before determining whether an implant-supported solution is appropriate.

DENTAL IMPLANT PROCESS

What May Be Involved in Dental Implant Treatment?

The treatment process is planned around the individual's dental condition. The exact stages and timing can vary from person to person.

01

Dental Assessment

The dental professional evaluates the teeth, gums, jawbone and overall oral condition.

02

Treatment Planning

The suitable tooth-replacement approach is planned according to the individual's needs.

03

Implant Placement

If an implant is considered suitable, the implant fixture may be placed into the jawbone.

04

Restoration

After the appropriate stages of healing and planning, a suitable restoration may be attached.

Dental Implant FAQs

Answers to common questions about dental implants and missing tooth replacement.

What are dental implants? +

Dental implants are a tooth-replacement option that uses an implant fixture placed in the jawbone to support a dental restoration. Suitability depends on professional evaluation.

Can a dental implant replace one missing tooth? +

A dental implant may be considered for replacing a single missing tooth. The gums, jawbone, remaining teeth and overall oral condition should be evaluated by a qualified dental professional.

What is involved in dental implant treatment? +

Treatment may involve assessment, planning, implant placement and restoration. The exact sequence and timing depend on the individual's dental condition.

Can dental implants replace multiple missing teeth? +

Dental implants can be considered for multiple missing teeth and some full-arch tooth-replacement situations. The appropriate approach must be determined by a qualified dental professional.
