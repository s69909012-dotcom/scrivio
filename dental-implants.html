UNDERSTANDING DENTAL IMPLANTS

What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots placed in the jawbone to support a replacement tooth or dental restoration. They can be considered for people with missing teeth, depending on their individual dental condition.

HOW AN IMPLANT WORKS

The Main Parts of a Dental Implant

01

Implant Fixture

The implant fixture acts as an artificial tooth root and is placed within the jawbone.

02

Abutment

The abutment connects the implant fixture with the replacement tooth or restoration.

03

Crown or Restoration

The visible restoration replaces the missing tooth and is designed to provide a natural-looking result.

TREATMENT PROCESS

How Does Dental Implant Treatment Work?

Dental implant treatment can involve several stages. The exact approach depends on the patient's individual dental condition and treatment plan.

01

Assessment

The dental condition and suitability for implant treatment are evaluated.

02

Treatment Planning

A suitable treatment approach is planned based on the individual situation.

03

Implant Placement

The implant fixture is placed in the jawbone by a qualified dental professional.

04

Restoration

After the appropriate treatment stages, the replacement tooth or restoration is fitted.

TREATMENT OPTIONS

Dental Implant Options

Different implant-supported solutions may be considered depending on the number of missing teeth and individual treatment requirements.

Single Tooth Implant

An implant-supported option that may be considered when replacing a single missing tooth.

Multiple Tooth Replacement

Implant-supported solutions may be considered when several teeth are missing.

Full-Mouth or Full-Arch Options

People with extensive tooth loss may explore implant-supported full-mouth or full-arch options.

POTENTIAL BENEFITS

Why Consider Dental Implants?

Designed to support a fixed tooth-replacement restoration.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Dental Implant FAQs

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots placed in the jawbone to support a replacement tooth or dental restoration.

Who can get dental implants?

Dental implants may be considered by people with missing teeth, but suitability depends on individual dental and oral health conditions.

Are dental implants suitable for everyone?

No. Suitability can depend on factors such as gum health, jawbone condition and individual dental circumstances.

How long does dental implant treatment take?

Treatment time varies between patients and depends on the treatment plan, healing requirements and individual dental circumstances.

How much do dental implants cost?

Dental implant costs vary depending on the number of teeth being replaced, treatment complexity, restoration type and other individual factors.

Are dental implants better than dentures?

Dental implants and dentures are different tooth replacement options. The appropriate option depends on individual dental needs and professional assessment.
