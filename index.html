DENTAL IMPLANT TREATMENT

Understanding Dental Implants

Dental implants are a tooth-replacement option for people with one or more missing teeth. A typical implant restoration may include an implant fixture, an abutment and a dental crown.

The appropriate treatment approach depends on each person's dental condition and is determined by a qualified dental professional.

What Are Dental Implants?

A tooth-replacement option used to replace missing teeth.

Missing Tooth Replacement

Dental implants are one option a professional may consider for replacing a missing tooth.

Treatment Process

Assessment, planning, implant placement and restoration may be involved.

Cost Factors

Costs can vary according to the individual treatment plan.
