WHAT WE DO

What Is FindToCare?

FindToCare is a patient-enquiry platform focused on helping people take the first step toward finding relevant dental care.

01

Explore

02

Enquire

03

Connect

Where applicable, relevant and qualified enquiries may be shared with a suitable partner clinic or hospital for follow-up.

OUR PURPOSE

Why FindToCare Exists

Finding information about missing teeth and dental implants can sometimes feel confusing. There can be different treatment approaches, different clinical considerations and many questions before taking the next step.

FindToCare is designed to make that first step easier by bringing useful treatment information and patient enquiries together in one simple platform.

A simpler starting point

Explore information, tell us what you are looking for and, where applicable, connect with a relevant dental care provider.

OUR ROLE

Information First. Clinical Decisions by Dental Professionals.

FindToCare provides treatment information and facilitates patient enquiries. We do not diagnose dental conditions or decide which treatment is appropriate for an individual.

Treatment recommendations and clinical decisions are made by qualified dental professionals at the relevant clinic or hospital.
