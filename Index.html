Dental implant information

What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are used to provide an implant-supported foundation for replacing missing teeth. Depending on an individual's dental condition and treatment requirements, different implant-supported solutions may be considered.

Treatment considerations

When You May Consider Dental Implants

Dental implant treatment may be considered for different types of missing-tooth situations. The appropriate option depends on individual clinical assessment.

Missing One Tooth

Explore a dental implant-supported replacement option for an individual missing tooth.

Multiple Missing Teeth

Learn about implant-supported options when several teeth are missing.

Extensive Tooth Loss

Explore full-arch or full-mouth implant-supported treatment approaches where clinically appropriate.
