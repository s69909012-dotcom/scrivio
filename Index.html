What are dental implants?

Dental implants are tooth replacement solutions that can be used to replace missing teeth. Treatment planning depends on the individual's dental condition and treatment requirements.

How a dental implant works

A typical implant restoration may include an implant fixture, an abutment and a dental crown. The implant fixture is placed in the jawbone and the restoration is attached after the required treatment stages.

Who may consider implants?

People with missing teeth may consider implant treatment. Suitability depends on factors such as oral health, available bone, overall dental condition and the treatment plan recommended by a dental professional.

Dental implant treatment options

Different implant solutions may be considered depending on the number of missing teeth and individual treatment needs.

01

Single Tooth Implant

An implant-based option for replacing an individual missing tooth with a dental restoration.

02

Multiple Teeth

Implant treatment may also be planned for people with multiple missing teeth.

03

Full Arch Implants

Full-arch implant treatment may be considered when many or all teeth are missing, depending on clinical suitability.

Dental implant treatment process

The exact process varies by patient and treatment plan.

1

Assessment

The dental team evaluates the teeth, gums, jawbone and overall oral condition.

2

Planning

A treatment plan is prepared based on the patient's individual requirements.

3

Implant Placement

The implant fixture may be placed as part of the planned treatment.

4

Restoration

The final restoration is fitted after the required stages of treatment.

How much do dental implants cost?

Dental implant cost can vary significantly depending on the number of implants, implant system, restoration, additional procedures and individual treatment requirements.

Online search volumes and general price information cannot determine the exact treatment cost for an individual. A dental clinic should confirm the final treatment plan and cost after evaluation.
