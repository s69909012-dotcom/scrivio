Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how FindToCare may collect, use and handle information submitted through our website and dental implant enquiry forms.

Last updated: August 2026

1. About FindToCare

FindToCare is a patient-enquiry platform focused on helping people explore dental implant treatment information and submit treatment enquiries.

FindToCare does not itself provide dental diagnosis or dental treatment. Where applicable, information submitted through an enquiry may be shared with a relevant partner clinic, hospital or dental professional for further communication regarding the enquiry.

2. Information We May Collect

When you submit an enquiry through our website, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your location or area
  • Your dental treatment requirement
  • Your preferred contact method
  • Your preferred treatment timeline
  • Other information that you voluntarily provide through an enquiry

You should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive personal information through the enquiry form.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through FindToCare may be used to:

  • Review and understand your enquiry
  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Communicate with you using your preferred contact method
  • Understand the treatment information you are looking for
  • Where applicable, connect or share your enquiry with a relevant partner clinic, hospital or dental professional
  • Improve our website, enquiry process and services
  • Maintain records relating to enquiries and communications
  • Prevent misuse, spam or fraudulent activity

4. Sharing Your Information

FindToCare may share information submitted through an enquiry with a relevant partner clinic, hospital or dental professional where this is necessary to respond to or process your treatment enquiry.

Information may also be shared with service providers who help us operate our website, enquiry forms, communication systems, hosting, analytics or other business functions, where reasonably necessary for those services.

We may also disclose information where required to do so by applicable law or to protect our legal rights, website, users or business.

5. Contact Preferences

If you select phone call, WhatsApp or both as your preferred contact method, FindToCare or an applicable partner may use the contact information you provide for communication related to your enquiry.

You may request that future communications stop by contacting us using the contact details provided on our website.

6. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use cookies, analytics tools and similar technologies to understand website usage, improve performance, maintain security and improve the user experience.

These technologies may collect information such as browser type, device information, pages visited, approximate location, referring pages and general website usage information.

We may use third-party analytics or website technologies where required for operating and improving the website.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted to us against unauthorised access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no website, online transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We may retain enquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, maintain business records, improve our services, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations or protect our legitimate interests.

9. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may contain links or integrations to third-party websites or services, including communication or social media platforms.

FindToCare is not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any third-party service you choose to use.

10. Your Choices and Requests

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights or choices relating to your personal information, including requesting access, correction or deletion of certain information.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact FindToCare using the contact information provided on our website.

11. Children's Information

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children without appropriate involvement of a parent or legal guardian.

If you believe that a child has submitted personal information to us without appropriate permission, please contact us so that the information can be reviewed and, where appropriate, removed.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, services, business practices or applicable legal requirements.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how FindToCare handles information submitted through the website, you can contact us through the Contact page.
