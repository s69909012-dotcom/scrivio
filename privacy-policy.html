Privacy Policy

How FindToCare collects, uses, and protects information submitted through our website.

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry through our website, we may collect information such as:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • WhatsApp number
  • Location or area
  • Treatment requirement
  • Preferred contact method
  • Information included in your message

We may also collect basic website usage information, such as browser type, device information, and pages visited.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Understand your treatment requirement
  • Help you explore relevant dental treatment options
  • Contact you through your selected contact method
  • Improve our website and enquiry process

3. Sharing Your Information

If appropriate and with your enquiry in mind, information you provide may be shared with a relevant dental clinic or healthcare provider to help address your treatment enquiry.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Communication

By submitting an enquiry, you agree that FindToCare may contact you regarding your enquiry through the contact method you selected.

You can choose Phone Call, WhatsApp, or Both when submitting the form.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information submitted through our website. However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to understand website usage, improve performance, and measure how visitors interact with the website.

7. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for website hosting, analytics, communication, forms, or other website functions. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

8. Your Choices

You may choose not to provide certain information. However, some information may be necessary for us to respond to or process your enquiry.

If you want to ask about information you have submitted to us, you can contact FindToCare using the contact details provided on our Contact page.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for treatment enquiries.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact FindToCare using the contact details available on our Contact page.
